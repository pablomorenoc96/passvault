# Humanizer: Remove AI Writing Patterns

Rewrite AI-sounding text so it reads like a human developer or writer, not a chatbot. Do not change what it says or invent details.

## Core Rules

1. **Eliminate AI Buzzwords:** Never use words like *delve*, *testament*, *pivotal*, *crucial*, *tapestry*, *vibrant*, *showcase*, *intricate*, *enhance*, *groundbreaking*, *military-grade*, *battle-tested*.
2. **Simple Verbs over Fancy Verbs:** Replace *serves as*, *stands as*, *boasts*, *features* with simple verbs: *is*, *has*, *includes*.
3. **No "Not X but Y" Clichés:** Avoid phrases like "It's not just a tool, it's a statement" or "Not only... but also...".
4. **No Forced Triads:** AI loves forcing thoughts into groups of three ("innovation, efficiency, and scale"). State points directly.
5. **No Em-dash Overuse:** Avoid excessive em dashes (—). Use standard punctuation (periods, commas, parentheses).
6. **No Artificial Enthusiasm:** Remove excessive exclamations, emojis on every bullet point, or marketing-style cheerleading.
7. **Honest Limitations:** Human engineers always state what their tool does *not* do. Keep expectations realistic.
8. **Direct, Conversational Flow:** Sound like an engineer talking to another engineer on a technical forum.
